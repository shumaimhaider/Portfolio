# Profile Image Instructions

## Place Your Profile Photo Here

1. **File Name:** Save your profile picture as `profile.jpg`
2. **Location:** Place it in this folder: `/public/images/profile.jpg`
3. **Recommended Specs:**
   - **Format:** JPG or WebP (optimized)
   - **Size:** 800x800px (square, 1:1 aspect ratio)
   - **File Size:** Under 200KB (compress with TinyPNG.com if needed)
   - **Quality:** High resolution for retina displays

## Image Guidelines

- ✅ Professional headshot
- ✅ Clear, well-lit background
- ✅ Friendly, confident expression
- ✅ Business casual or professional attire
- ✅ Square crop (will be displayed as circular)

## Compression Tools

- **TinyPNG:** https://tinypng.com
- **Squoosh:** https://squoosh.app
- **ImageOptim:** For Mac users

Once you add `profile.jpg` to this folder, it will automatically appear in your portfolio!
